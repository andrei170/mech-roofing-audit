Why The Grade Is A C And What That Means For Price
You are not the weakest site in your market and you should not be sold as if you were

On seven measures you match or beat firms ranking around you. Your structured data is as complete as anyone's in this comparison. Somebody built this properly.

You lose on exactly three things, and they are one job: GA4, Tag Manager and conversion tracking. That is an afternoon, not a project, and it is the only place the competition is genuinely ahead.

Three jobs a week to six is not a website problem. You have the crew and you have the site. What you do not have is any record of which enquiry came from where — which is also why a previous lead supplier could tell you whatever they liked and you had no way to check it.

The Order We Would Do It In
  • Strip the dead classic analytics and install GA4 with conversion tracking. Calls and forms counted as conversions. This closes the only real gap to your competitors.
  • Pair the Meta pixel with outcome data so the audience you are already building becomes usable.
  • Rewrite the homepage into ten to fifteen subheadings. Same words, arranged as the questions a homeowner actually asks.
  • Extend the service-area pages across the rest of Rutherford, Polk and Henderson counties on the pattern you already have.
  • Put any manufacturer certification at the top, where Woot puts theirs.
  • Then paid, with conversions counted, aimed only at towns that have a page behind them.

Every firm we could measure around you knows what their advertising does. You do not.

That is the whole gap, and it is the cheapest thing on this list.
