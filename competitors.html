Every Competitor We Could Measure Runs Google Ads Conversion Tracking. You Are The Only One Who Does Not.

Three western NC roofers, measured exactly as you were. This market is more sophisticated than most.

We audit a lot of contractors. In almost every market, nobody is tracking conversions and the opportunity is to be the first. Here, all three readable competitors already are.

That makes measurement urgent rather than clever.

Three columns, and they are the only three you lose

Description, canonical, schema, pixel, click-to-call — you match or beat the field on all of them. Your word count is second-highest of the four.

GA4, Tag Manager and conversion tracking are the three you are missing, and all three competitors have all three. Plus the subheading gap, which is structural rather than technical.

Finding 1 — Even The Smallest Competitor Is Measuring
Woot Roofing 855 words · 1 subheading · 18 years wootroofing.com/forest-city-nc/ GA4 + Tag Manager + Ads conversion tag Owens Corning Preferred · 50-year warranty · seamless gutters
This is the one to look at hardest, because they are directly on your ground

Woot has a page built specifically for Forest City — your town — with fewer words and fewer subheadings than you, and they still run the full measurement stack.

They have less content and better information. They know which of those 855 words produces a phone call and you do not know which of your 1,026 does.

They also lead with the Owens Corning Preferred status and a 50-year warranty. If you hold a manufacturer certification, it should be as visible on your site as theirs is on theirs.

Finding 2 — WNC And Secure Show What The Subheading Gap Costs
WNC Roofing 1,057 words · 17 H2 · full stack + pixel Secure Roofing 1,272 words · 14 H2 · full stack + pixel Mech Roofing 1,026 words · 2 H2
Almost identical word counts. Completely different pages.

WNC publishes thirty-one more words than you and splits them across seventeen sections. That is not more writing — it is the same amount of writing, organised.

Each subheading is a question answered: what a new roof costs, how long it takes, whether insurance covers it, what happens with the old shingles. A homeowner scanning on a phone lands on the one that matters to them.

This is the cheapest content win available to you, because the material is already on your page.

Finding 3 — Where You Already Match Or Beat Them
Structured data you publish RoofingContractor AND WebSite, with a full street address -- as good as any Meta description specific to Forest City, not generic Canonical correct Click-to-call 4 links -- more than most of the field Meta pixel live (Woot does not have one) Word count 2nd of 4 Service range roofing, gutters, decks, insurance work, pressure washing, gallery, recent projects
Worth saying plainly

You are not the weakest site in this market. On seven measures you are level with or ahead of firms ranking around you, and your schema is as complete as anyone's.

The gap is narrow and specific. That is a much better position than a site that needs rebuilding, and it means the work is cheap.

What This Field Tells Us To Do
  • Close the measurement gap first. GA4, Tag Manager, conversion tracking. Three competitors have all three and it is the only place you are genuinely behind.
  • Restructure the homepage to WNC's shape — ten to seventeen subheadings, same words.
  • Put any manufacturer certification where Woot puts theirs, at the top.
  • Extend the service-area pages. You have three, the pattern is right, and no competitor has locked up the smaller towns.
  • Then paid, once conversions are counted, aimed at towns that have a page behind them.

You match this field on seven measures and lose on three.

All three are the same fix, and it is an afternoon.
